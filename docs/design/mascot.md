# Magpie mascot

A cheerful magpie carrying a golden grocery bag: a little companion for collecting everything in one trip. The black-and-white bird, teal wing, yellow focal accent, rounded silhouette, and bold edges follow `.tmp/design-system/project/README.md` and its color tokens.

Assets live in `public/icons/`: `magpie-mascot.png` is the original artwork; `icon-128.png`, `icon-192.png`, and `icon-512.png` are square PNG application icons. They have an opaque paper background. These are standard icons, not maskable icons. The application has not yet been scaffolded, so manifest integration is deferred.

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool, then resized with Lanczos resampling.

`public/icons/magpie-mascot.svg` is a scalable vector trace of the original artwork, with 18 filled paths and no embedded bitmap. It uses a 1254×1254 viewBox and defaults to 128×128 display size. Tracing simplifies the original's subtle shading into flat colors. The SVG was parsed and rendered for visual verification.

## Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: Magpie grocery shopping application mascot and square app icon, optimized for 128x128 pixels.
Primary request: A fun, cheeky, friendly magpie mascot carrying a single golden yellow grocery bag, a confident little shopping companion. Black head and body, white belly and cheek, broad teal wing, long recognizable magpie tail, lively large eye, short black beak. Rounded chunky geometric silhouette and expressive slight head tilt. Bird and bag form one compact unmistakable mark.
Design-system reference: Magpie is bright and playful, fun through color and shape, never clutter. Chunky quirky shapes, round joins, short hard sticker drop instead of blurred shadows. Exact palette: ink #121521, paper #f3f5fa, white #ffffff, sheen teal #00a88f, trinket yellow #ffc21a. Bag is the single yellow focal accent, outlined in ink.
Style: polished flat graphic mascot illustration, bold smooth contours, minimal internal detail, no gradients, no texture, no 3D.
Composition: single centered mascot on full-bleed solid paper #f3f5fa square background. Entire bird including tail and bag visible, generous 12 percent margin, strong legibility when downscaled to 128px. No icon grid or mockup. No text, letters, watermark, extra objects or decoration.
